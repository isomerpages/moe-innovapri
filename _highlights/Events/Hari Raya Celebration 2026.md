<p>The school community as well as parent volunteers came together in joyous
celebration of Hari Raya Aidilfitri on Monday, 9 March, with a lively and
engaging concert by Sri Warisan. The performance captivated both pupils
and staff, offering not only entertainment but also a meaningful cultural
experience. Pupils were introduced to traditional Malay musical instruments
and learned about their importance in bringing Malay songs to life. The
festive atmosphere was further enhanced as everyone joined in singing cheerful
Malay songs, filling the hall with energy and excitement. Adding to the
fun, teachers and pupils were invited on stage to dance along, creating
unforgettable moments and truly capturing the spirit of Hari Raya.</p>
