<p><strong>Innova Primary School Celebrates World Book Day &amp; Earth Day 2026</strong>
</p>
<p>Innova Primary School was filled with excitement and creativity as students
and staff came together to celebrate World Book Day (WBD) and Earth Day
through a week of engaging activities centered on reading, storytelling,
and environmental awareness.</p>
<p>On Monday, 20 April, students and staff dressed in blue and green to show
appreciation for our Earth and kick-start the celebrations. The school
was filled with vibrant colours as everyone came together to support the
Earth Day message.</p>

<p>Leading up to the celebrations, students participated in the <strong>Family Storytime Showdown</strong>,
where families submitted creative video performances based on their favourite
stories. Students also took part in the <strong>“From Trash to Treasure: The Journey of an Item” Writing Challenge</strong>,
where they transformed discarded household items into imaginative stories
about recycling and upcycling while incorporating important iSTAR values.</p>

<p>To promote sustainable habits, students contributed pre-loved books for
the <strong>“Share a Book, Save the Earth” Book Exchange Initiative</strong>,
encouraging the values of sharing, reusing, and giving books a new life.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>A key highlight of the celebrations was the assembly programme held on
Thursday, 23 April, featuring invited guest speaker <strong>Mr Neil Humphreys</strong>.
Through his engaging storytelling session themed <em>“Say Yes To Waste Less”</em>,
students learned meaningful lessons about environmental responsibility
and the importance of reducing waste.</p>
<p></p>

<p>Students also enjoyed the much-anticipated <strong>Book Character Costume Parade</strong>,
where every class selected two representatives to showcase creative costumes
inspired by their favourite storybook characters. Many costumes were thoughtfully
designed using recycled and reused materials, reflecting both creativity
and environmental awareness.</p>

and Q&amp;A challenges about reducing waste and recycling.</p>
